# Screens: structural wireframes

> **Supporting file of the Render UI spec** ([`../spec.md`](../spec.md)). It is copied from the design workbench (`~/dev/render-ui-design/design/`) as of the final phase.
> - **Phase tags** ("phase 3", "phase 6") mark when a rule was decided. Decision records (D…, F…, A…, challenges, evaluations) are workbench history, not requirements.
> - **File names** in code spans map like this: `visual-language.md`, `grammar.md` and `stack.md` → [`../reference/`](../reference/); `wireframes.md` → [`../wireframes/layout.md`](../wireframes/layout.md); `screens.md` → [`../wireframes/text-mocks.md`](../wireframes/text-mocks.md).
> - **Paths** under `experiments/`, `inputs/` and `evaluation-*.md` are in the workbench (`~/dev/render-ui-design/`).
> - **Measured, proposed and pending** claims are separated in [`../evidence.md`](../evidence.md).


**These are structural wireframes, not the visual design.** They fix what each screen shows, where, in what order, and with what real content.

The visual design (Ember palette with accent roles and tool-call tints, the reading/side surface split, Iosevka for code and Iosevka Etoile for chrome and prose, Carbon icons, spacing, surfaces and motion) is specified in `visual-language.md`. **Pixel layouts at 1512 × 982 are in `wireframes.md`**, which is the basis for the tldraw wireframes. The rendered mockups come after those.

The text grids are 160 columns (100 for the compact variant) only because a monospace grid is a convenient way to draw structure. Every row in every block is exactly the declared width, generated by `tools/build_screens.py`. Partial blocks say which region they replace. View with wrapping off.

**Reading rules hold even in wireframes.**
- Prose is generated by the same word-wrap rules as the client (`visual-language.md` §4.2), self-checked by the WRAP check in `tools/mock_lib.py`.
- Paths truncate to `…/leaf`.
- IDs are never cut.

**Content.**
- **Real, from the ledger:**
  - the slices and their states on 2026-10-27;
  - the `execute-symbolic-calls` blocking decision Q1, obligations B1–B5/A1–A6, findings W1/F12–F17, commits `5712d1a` `06500de` `69ca7f9` and the gate counts;
  - the `simplify-adapter-launch-tests` hold (10-19);
  - `work-router` (hold lifted 10-23) and `execute-causal-scan` (decided 10-21) shown as not needing you;
  - 1 eligible `native-engine` slice;
  - downstream counts from the ledger graph as the phase-3 fake host computes them (`fake-host/report-phase3.md`): `execute-symbolic-calls` 4 direct / 25 transitive, `execute-causal-scan` 3 / 23, `evaluate-exact-sum-with-demand` 3 / 23, `specify-rsps-profile` 2 / 4, `specify-cma-profiles` 8 / 29. Queue columns show the transitive count. Phase 2's 34 was a hand count;
  - proposal small multiples and SC-16 counts from the host's slices scope (RQ-41): 72 open, 26 merged, 4 superseded, 63 waiting on a dependency, 3 ready.
- **Real, from `docs/spec-review-notes.md`:** the human's annotations A006/A014–A022.
- **Fixture, invented:**
  - the `render-host` design workflow and its digests;
  - assignment IDs, ages, sequence numbers and times;
  - the coordinator transcript (composed from real document text; the ledger holds no transcripts);
  - the quarantine case and `op9`.

**One fixture timeline**, so the screens agree with each other:
- 13:57: `modules r2` is emitted.
- 14:01: `@7` is saved (annotations v3).
- 14:03: queue (SC-02).
- 14:05: review with 2 local drafts (SC-04).
- 14:06:55: your desktop saves `@8` (v4).
- Then **either** the laptop already has v4, so the readback opens STALE before anything is sent (SC-09d), **or** it has not, so the host answers `stale_basis` (SC-10b). These are the two orderings of H4, not one sequence.
- 14:07:10: after reading `@8` and saving the 2 drafts (v5), the approve readback binds v5 (SC-09b).

**Wireframe conventions:**
- `›` in column 1 = cursor row; `▌` = text cursor; `[a]` = a hint chip.
- `[N]`, `[I]` and `[◆]` = the statusline mode badge.
- Bracketed marks (`[+]`, `[!]`, `[?]`, `[×]`) and the glyphs `◆ ✗ ? ▸ # ↺ · ✓ ×` stand for the icons in `visual-language.md` §8. They are also the TUI fallback.
- Colour follows the marks: coral only on `◆` that needs you now.

Each screen lists the tasks it serves (`task-inventory.md`), the failures it defends (`failure-catalogue.md`) and the protocol fields it needs (`protocol-requirements.md`).

## SC-03 Chat reader with modal prompt and steering

- Authors come from host metadata in the gutter and header band, never from message text (FC-S10).
- Prose wraps at words within the 68 ch measure.
- Tool calls fold to one line. They have no icon: the tint shows the outcome, and the outcome word sits right-aligned (bold for failed and unknown; `visual-language.md` §5.5). A text grid cannot draw tints, so `[ok tint]` marks them.
- References the host resolved (`A020`, `A021`, `M3`) render in gold Iosevka with a solid underline, which a text grid cannot show either (`visual-language.md` §5.9). Code blocks are syntax-highlighted (§5.10).
- The prompt header says **before** you send whether `⏎` prompts or steers.
- A steer that arrives after the turn ended is refused with `turn_ended`, kept as a draft, and offered as a follow-up (RQ-09).
