# Render

Render is a personal development workflow authority: a human can leave and return to explicit commitments, attributable evidence and a known next action. This glossary uses the language of the authoritative [accepted specification](docs/spec.md); it does not replace that design.

## Language

**Workflow**: A retained journey from a human goal toward a finding, experimental evidence or accepted development work. Conversations and clients participate in a Workflow but do not own its authority.

**Ground**: The retained human goal, constraints, facts, uncertainty and task shape from which a Workflow proceeds. An assistant suggestion is not itself an agreed human constraint.

**Checkpoint**: A requested design decision over an exact emission, its annotations and required evidence. Its captured routing distinguishes a human decision from permitted delegated technical advancement.

**Emission**: Immutable checkpoint content with its structural manifest and exact review basis. Saved human edits are retained concerns, not a new approved Emission.

**Annotation**: An attributable saved human concern about an Emission. A subsequent disposition explains how the concern was acted on, considered or dismissed.

**Proposal**: A coherent set of explicit development commitments and their Slices and Dependencies. Proposal integration is distinct from delivery to main.

**Slice**: A bounded, independently reviewable part of a Proposal with an accepted Contract. Its Dependencies name the qualifying prior work required before it can proceed.

**Contract revision**: An immutable accepted set of obligations for a Slice. Once consumed by an Assignment, changed obligations require supersession and replacement rather than editing that revision.

**Assignment**: A role's authorized ownership of work with exact inputs and a current generation. Ownership is distinct from an executing process or an available execution slot.

**Attempt**: One execution under an Assignment's authority. Replacing an Attempt does not erase obligations, history or review counts.

**Implementation pass**: One authorized implementation interval ending at at most one accepted sealed-source handoff. Same-pass Audit fixes remain inside it; authorized post-handoff implementation opens a successor even when resulting bytes match.

**Audit**: One logical Standards-and-Contracts review invocation per Implementation pass, with fresh independent contexts inspecting the actual pre-fix source. Audit findings are fixed or disposed before handoff; Audit is neither a Watchdog verdict nor an inner re-review loop.

**Prepared source**: The exact source combining a sealed implementation result with the observed proposal target, subject to required verification before Watchdog. A clean combination alone is not a behavioral pass.

**Watchdog**: The independent review of exact Prepared source against accepted obligations, verification and prior findings. Every completed Watchdog review counts, including passing drift reviews; interruption is not another completed review.

**Qualifying integration**: A verified and recorded incorporation of exact reviewed source into the authoritative proposal target. It clears eligible same-proposal Dependencies; neither a review verdict nor a forge label substitutes for it.

**Record**: The Workflow phase that prepares approved repository knowledge under accepted obligations and the normal reviewed source-delivery path. It is not another authority store.

**Artifact**: Retained immutable content with identity and provenance. A temporary result path or content digest alone is not recoverable evidence.

**Evidence**: Retained observations and supporting material attributed to the source, obligations, verifier, role and observation actually involved. Model assertions, captured execution results and human observations are distinct kinds of evidence.

**Approval**: An authenticated human decision on an exact basis. Saved edits, silence, tool output and machine technical advancement are not human Approval.

**Delegated technical decision**: Machine-attributed advancement of an eligible Seams or Modules Checkpoint under its captured settings and agreed human scope. It cannot accept final Contracts, resolve new human product choices or merge main.

**Operation**: An identified intent and its attempts to cause an effect outside the accepting transaction. Admission means the effect may already occur; an uncertain outcome is not proof it was unsent.

**Protected cutoff**: The latest acknowledged-work cutoff actually covered by a verified off-host recovery generation. A scheduled backup is not protection, and outage exposure is not bounded by the healthy target.

## Example dialogue

**Human:** Watchdog requested rework. Can the implementer reuse the previous Audit?

**Developer:** No. Authorized implementation rework opens a new Implementation pass with its own Audit. Fixing that new Audit's findings stays in the same pass. Replacing an interrupted Attempt alone would not open another pass.

**Human:** The next Watchdog passed. Is the dependent Slice unblocked?

**Developer:** Only after Qualifying integration is recorded. Forge publication may lag without blocking eligible local work; main merge remains yours.
